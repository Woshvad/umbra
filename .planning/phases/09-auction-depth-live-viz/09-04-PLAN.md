<objective>
Ship the privacy-safe live price-discovery surface (AUCT-03 + VIZ-01): the solver computes an AGGREGATE indicative clearing price, net imbalance, and estimated matched volume server-side from the whole sealed batch and publishes SCALARS ONLY during the open window (small-N guarded), and the hand-rolled `CrossingChart` gains an assembling→locked distinction so the curve builds as orders seal and the red p* LOCKS at close. This runs entirely on the operator plane, off the settlement path.

Purpose: This is a genuine privacy surface, not a nicer chart — an over-eager feed that broadcast raw candidate prices (each candidate price = an individual order's limit) would re-introduce the leak the sealed Order prevents. Scalars-only + the ≥2-order small-N guard + coarse bins during open are the mitigations. It reuses the existing pure `choosePStar`/`matchedAt` and `lib/curve.ts` unchanged.
Output: A scalars-only indicative block on `GET /round/:id` for the OPEN state, its api.test.ts privacy-guard tests, the `assembling|locked` CrossingChart, and the Theatre indicative panel.
</objective>
