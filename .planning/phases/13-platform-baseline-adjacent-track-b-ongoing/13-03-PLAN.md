<objective>
Deliver OPS-03 as two pure, standalone solver modules: an idempotency store + Express middleware that dedupes mutating POSTs by `(Idempotency-Key, sha256(canonical body))`, and a round-lifecycle finite state machine that rejects illegal transitions. Both are unit-tested standalone here; wiring the middleware and guard into the Express app happens in plan 13-07. The ledger `Round.status` stays authoritative — these are API-layer guards.

Purpose: make order submission and settlement idempotent (a retry never double-submits) and prevent a bad client from driving an illegal round sequence.
Output: `idempotency.ts` (+test), `fsm.ts` (+test).
</objective>
