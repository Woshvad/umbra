02The Problem

The order book is the leak.

A desk that wants to move a large position has nowhere to hide. The public book shows the intent, the market moves against it, and the cost lands in the fill. For decades, size and privacy have pulled in opposite directions. You trade small and slow, or you trade big and pay to be seen.

02 / 09
03The Product

One window. One price. No leaks.

Desks submit sealed orders for a tokenized bond during a short window. Nobody sees anyone else's order. When the window closes, an AI solver finds the single price that matches the most volume, and the whole batch settles in one transaction. Each desk learns only its own fill.

03 / 09
04The Flow

Bid, clear, settle.

01
Bid blind
Three desks submit into one batch. Each sees its own ticket and none of its rivals.
02

03

05The Edge

Three things a public market cannot give you.

Privacy that holds at the wire
Each desk speaks to the ledger as itself, so the ledger will not hand it a rival's order. Put each desk on its own node and the orders are never even stored in the same place.
An AI you do not have to trust
The model proposes the price and writes the reasoning. A plain algorithm and the contract on the ledger both recompute it. The trade settles only when all three agree.
Settlement with no gap
Two legs, one transaction. There is never a moment where one side has paid and the other has not delivered.

06The Foundation

Canton is the unlock.

Privacy is the default, not a feature
Canton shows each party only the parts of a deal they belong to. The guarantee comes from the network, not from a promise in our code.
One transaction moves everything
The bond and the cash settle together, with finality. No clearing house in the middle, no waiting for the next day.
The assets are real and the books are already true
When the batch clears on the ledger, settlement has happened. There is nothing left to reconcile.
